Delete Your Data

Last updated: October 4, 2026

You can permanently delete all of your logged history and health data while keeping your account, login, and goals intact. This is different from deleting your account entirely — see Delete Your Account if that's what you're looking for instead.

Your account stays active. Your profile, goals, and consent settings are not affected — only your historical data is erased.

1 If you still have the Juyo app

This is instant and handled entirely on your device:

  1. Open Juyo and go to Menu → Settings
  2. Scroll to the bottom, to the section next to Delete Account, and tap Delete All History (in red)
  3. Type DELETE to confirm
  4. Tap Delete All History

This takes effect immediately — there is no waiting period.

2 If you no longer have the app installed

Email us from the address associated with your account and we will process the deletion on our end:

Email privacy@hebare.com

Please include the email address you used to sign in with Juyo (Google or Apple), and mention that you'd like your data deleted while keeping your account. We will confirm once complete, typically within 30 days.

What gets deleted

This permanently removes:

  • All logged Nutrition, Activity, Mood, Sleep, and Mindfulness entries
  • All medication and medical history entries, if provided
  • Your Ask Juyo conversation history
  • All computed health metrics (Health Score history, Strain, Stress Levels, Cardio Load, and related records)

What stays

Your account itself is unaffected:

  • Your login (Google or Apple) and profile
  • Your goals and targets
  • Your consent settings in Data & Privacy

Because your data-type permissions (Settings → Data & Privacy) stay on, Juyo will simply start collecting fresh history again from this point forward.

This action cannot be undone. Once confirmed, deleted history cannot be recovered. This is an all-or-nothing action — we're currently not able to delete only a single data type (for example, only Sleep) while keeping the rest.

Questions
