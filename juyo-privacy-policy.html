Juyo

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026  ·  Effective for all users of the Juyo mobile application

Juyo is a health-tracking app published by Hebare LLC. This policy explains what information Juyo collects, how it's used, who it's shared with, and the choices you have. We wrote it to describe what the app actually does — not a generic template.

In short: Juyo collects the health, fitness, and account data needed to calculate your Health Score and power the in-app AI assistant. We never sell your data. A small number of service providers (listed below) process data on our behalf, strictly to run the app.

1. Who we are

Juyo is developed and operated by Hebare LLC ("Juyo," "we," "us"). This policy applies to the Juyo mobile app on iOS and Android, and to any related services we operate.

2. Information we collect

Account & profile information

When you create an account, we collect your age or date of birth, biological sex, height, weight, activity level, dietary preference, and the health goals you set.

Health & fitness data from your device

With your explicit, granular permission, Juyo reads the following data types from Apple Health (HealthKit) or Android Health Connect:

Data typeUsed for
Heart rateActivity & Sleep quadrants, Strain calculation
Heart rate variability (HRV)Mind quadrant, stress estimation
Sleep analysisSleep quadrant
Step countActivity quadrant
Blood oxygen (SpO2)Dashboard health signals
Electrocardiogram (ECG) classificationDashboard health signals
Body mass, body fat %, lean body massBody composition tracking
Blood glucoseGlucose tracking

You choose which of these to share during setup, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings. Denying a type does not block use of the app — the related quadrant simply shows no data until it's enabled.

Information you log yourself

  • Nutrition — meals, calories, and macro/micronutrient estimates you log via the Ask Juyo chat, including photos of food you choose to share (the image itself is analyzed and then discarded — it is not stored).
  • Activity — workouts you log manually or confirm from a connected wearable.
  • Mood and mindfulness — optional check-ins, where available.
  • Medical history (optional) — if you choose to share them, chronic conditions, prescription or over-the-counter medications, and supplements, so the AI assistant can give more relevant guidance. This is never required to use the app.

Your conversations with Juyo's AI assistant

Messages you send to Ask Juyo, and the context needed to answer them (your recent Health Score, quadrant data, and profile), are sent to our AI infrastructure to generate a response. See "Third parties" below for where this processing happens.

Device and diagnostic data

Like most apps, we automatically collect basic device and usage information (device type, operating system, crash logs, app events) to keep Juyo working reliably and to understand which features are used.

3. How we use your information

  • To calculate your Health Score and the four quadrant scores (Nutrition, Activity, Sleep, Mind)
  • To power the Ask Juyo AI assistant and generate personalized guidance
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the app, including diagnosing technical problems
  • To communicate with you about your account or the service
  • To meet legal obligations

We do not use your health data for advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles from it.

4. How your information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers below, each of whom processes it solely to help us run Juyo, under contractual confidentiality and security obligations:

ProviderPurpose
Amazon Web Services (AWS Bedrock)Hosting, and the AI model that powers Ask Juyo
SentryCrash reporting and technical error diagnostics
MixpanelProduct analytics (feature usage, not health data content)

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Juyo, our users, or the public.

5. Data security

We use industry-standard safeguards — including encryption in transit and access controls on our systems — to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we continually work to improve our protections.

6. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), the Washington My Health My Data Act, and Nevada SB 370, including the right to:

  • Know what personal information we have collected about you
  • Request a copy or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for specific health-data types at any time, in Settings
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information — Juyo does not sell personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of in this respect

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Data retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the service. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal data, except where we are required to retain it by law.

8. Children's privacy

Juyo is not directed to, and is not intended for use by, anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you in the app or by other reasonable means before the change takes effect.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to privacy@hebare.com.
