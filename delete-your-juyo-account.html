Delete Your Account

Last updated: October 4, 2026

You can permanently delete your Juyo account and all associated data at any time. There are two ways to do this, depending on whether you still have the app installed.

1 If you still have the Juyo app

Deletion is instant and handled entirely on your device:

  1. Open Juyo and go to Menu → Settings
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account (in red)
  3. Type DELETE to confirm
  4. Tap Delete account permanently

This takes effect immediately — there is no waiting period.

2 If you no longer have the app installed

Email us from the address associated with your account and we will process the deletion on our end:

Email privacy@hebare.com

Please include the email address you used to sign in with Juyo (Google or Apple). We will confirm once the deletion is complete, typically within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your profile, goals, and account details
  • All logged Nutrition, Activity, Mood, Sleep, and Mindfulness entries
  • All medication and medical history entries, if provided
  • Your Ask Juyo conversation history
  • All computed health metrics (Health Score history, Strain, Stress Levels, and related records)
  • Your consent records
This action cannot be undone. Once confirmed, your data cannot be recovered. Signing back in afterward — even with the same Google or Apple account — creates a brand-new account with no history.

Retention after deletion

We do not keep a copy of your personal data after deletion, except where we are legally required to retain certain records (for example, limited financial or compliance records), in which case retention is limited to what the law requires.
